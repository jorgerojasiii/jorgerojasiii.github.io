About Me U-05

Electronics Engineer with a builder's mindset

I'm Jorge R. Rojas III, a licensed Electronics Engineer from the Technological University of the Philippines - Manila. My work bridges hardware, software, and AI — from designing embedded systems to building full-stack web applications and evaluating LLM models.

I thrive at the intersection of disciplines. Whether it's developing a medical AI agent (THERAPOD), automating an IoT attendance system, or speaking at Arduino Day about music box logic, I enjoy solving problems that require both depth and breadth.

Outside of engineering, I mentor, speak at tech events, and lead technical communities as former CTO of Google Developer Student Clubs - TUP Manila. I believe that the best innovations happen when different fields collide — and I've built my career around that philosophy.

From competitive programming to embedded systems, from AI evaluation to circuit design, I approach every project with curiosity and a commitment to quality. I'm always looking for the next challenge that pushes me to learn, grow, and build something meaningful.
